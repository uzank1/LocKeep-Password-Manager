Password Requirements

  • ❌ At least 14 characters
  • ❌ At least one uppercase letter (A–Z)
  • ❌ At least one lowercase letter (a–z)
  • ❌ At least one number (0–9)
  • ❌ At least one special character (!@#$%…)
CRITICAL: Your Master Password is the absolute key to your vault. LocKeep operates on a strict Zero-Knowledge architecture. If you forget this password, your data cannot be recovered by anyone. Keep it strictly safe.
